## 6. Corrections to section 7 found by the build

Each was measured on the restored dump (counts only) and is now in `docs/10` section 7 and the code.

1. **The cash label is `name`, not `source`.** On cash rows the old application keeps the label
   the user typed in `name` ("Cash Received" by default, 98 rows with their own); `source` is
   set on 57 rows and always repeats `name`. 7.3 row 14 said `note = source`, which would have
   dropped 41 labels. The cash look-alikes of question 100, counted with `name` as the label,
   are 4 for 135,200, the documented figure (with `source` they would be 5 for 154,200: a pair
   of 19,000 with different labels).
2. **Credit notes carry no payee.** `source` and `name` hold the generic "Credit Note" on all
   six; what each was for is in `description` ("Insurance …"). 7.3 said `payee = source`; the
   payee is now left empty when it is only that label, and the note keeps the description.
3. **The stored daily balances do not agree "until 16 September" without exception.** One of
   the two stored rows on a day without entries (28 December 2025) reads 23,612 above the
   entries, and 29 December is back in line; from 17 September they differ on 14 days. The
   exceptions list has a row for each day the gap changes: the three the letter asks for
   (−577,627, −577,627, −805,707) and the 28/29 December blip (+23,612, −23,612), 5 rows. The
   extract's printed "first differs" is 28 December 2025, 15 days.
4. **Rules made precise** where 7.3 left a case open (none occurs on the dump): two spellings of
   a sacco become one company spelled as on its earliest vehicle; a day with two different
   revenue amounts holds back every row of the day; a deduction or a petty-cash expense whose
   amount is not exact in whole cents for its quantity and unit price is held back (the
   target's generated total would round it); a copy without its original, or a committed
   expense without its copy, stops the transform; a soft-deleted row or a database that
   answers a write stops the extract; a legacy role with no permission gives a Fleet manager
   with every default denied; `updated_by` is the reviewer for approved expenses, the holder
   for waiting ones and credit notes, the migrated-by person for cash.
5. **Figures that stand.** Every other figure of 7.1, 7.5 and 7.6 and of questions 90 to 100
   equals the rehearsal's (section 3), including question 100's 12 expenses and 4 cash
   entries and the 180 held back.
